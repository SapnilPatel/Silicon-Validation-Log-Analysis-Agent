`BGL_2k.log` is the 2,000-line sample of the BlueGene/L supercomputer log from
[Loghub](https://github.com/logpai/loghub) (Zenodo record 8196385), used here as a
real, out-of-distribution test input. Original data: Adam Oliner and Jon Stearley,
"What Supercomputers Say: A Study of Five System Logs", DSN 2007. The full 4.7 M
line dataset is fetched on demand by `svla import-bgl --download`.
