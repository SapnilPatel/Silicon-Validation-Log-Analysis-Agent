- **Rationale:** The first PCIe problem in the run is 'Refclk instability detected | ppm=587 pll_lock=0' at 11.033 s, which matches the catalogue warning sign for RC_PCIE_REFCLK_GATING. It is followed by four 'LTSSM -> Recovery' entries (rx_detect_fail) at 11.3-12.1 s, an AER corrected error (ReplayTimerTimeout) at 12.388 s, then link-training timeouts in Polling (12.635 s) and Recovery (13.944 s). Both firmware retries failed, and the run ended in FATAL at 14.570 s. This is the sequence the catalogue describes for a physical-layer or refclk fault. There are no signs of the other PCIe causes: no low lane-margin/eye_width messages, no ASPM L1 entry, no EQ phase failure. The retrieved 'similar' incidents are thermal, DDR and NoC failures and don't match this signature. There was also a separate thermal trip in the same run (fan at 1233 rpm against a 4057 target, 116 °C, emergency shutdown at 12.067 s). That is its own RC_FAN_PROFILE_BAD incident. Heat may have worsened the refclk PLL, but it does not explain the PCIe failure: the refclk loss of lock was logged at 11.033 s, before the first high-temperature warning at 11.506 s. The PCIe register dump is raw hex with no decode, so it neither confirms nor rules anything out. Confidence is not higher because the thermal event overlaps, and whether the refclk was gated or just drifting is not yet confirmed.

**Recommended actions:**

1. Turn off spread-spectrum clocking and any refclk gating or CLKREQ#-driven clock control, then repeat link training many times.
1. Rerun with the fan profile fixed and temperature held steady, to rule out heat causing the refclk PLL to lose lock (which would point back to RC_FAN_PROFILE_BAD).

### 000001:THERMAL_TRIP
- **Root cause:** The fan ran far below its profile target (1233 rpm vs 4057 rpm, about 30%) while the die heated up. The DVFS step-down alone could not remove the heat, so the die went past the 105C trip point (116C) and the system shut down.

- **Rationale:** Order of events: 11.506 s Temperature high t_c=90 (DVFS policy active) -> 11.543 s Fan speed below profile rpm=1233 target_rpm=4057 -> 11.813 s DVFS step-down from 1200 to 1000 MHz, about 300 ms after the warning -> 12.060 s trip at t_c=116 -> 12.067 s emergency shutdown. The fan warning is the catalogue tell-tale for RC_FAN_PROFILE_BAD. Neither competing tell-tale appears anywhere in the run (WARN+ lines from 0 to 11.2 s and the full window before the trip): no 'power burst detected / p_w=' line, so RC_WORKLOAD_BURST is unsupported even though two similar runs had that cause; and no 'sensor-to-ambient delta / delta_c' line, so RC_HEATSINK_POOR is unsupported. The VCORE current also stayed flat at about 1.0-1.4 A, which argues against a burst. One caveat: the routine INFO temperature samples (sensors 0-3) stay at 23-27C right up to the trip, while the warnings report 90 and then 116C. Either the hotspot sensor is not in the routine samples, or the poor cooling is a contact problem. I have not ruled out heatsink/TIM contact, which is why confidence is not higher. The PCIe refclk instability (11.033 s), the LTSSM rx_detect_fail recoveries and the PCIe register dump (which is from 14.538 s, not the shutdown) look like a separate RC_PCIE_REFCLK_GATING issue, not the cause of this trip.

**Recommended actions:**
1. Check the fan curve / PWM duty mapping in the thermal policy at roughly 90C: is the commanded duty actually sent, and does it give the ~4000 rpm target?
1. Check tachometer feedback and fan supply voltage/current on this board; drive the fan at 100% PWM and confirm it reaches its rated rpm.
1. Re-run the same workload with the fan forced to the profile target (or with the fixed curve) and log the hotspot temperature to confirm it stays below 105C. If it still trips, re-seat the heatsink/TIM and check die-to-ambient delta.
1. Triage the PCIe refclk instability (pll_lock=0, repeated rx_detect_fail) separately as its own RC_PCIE_REFCLK_GATING issue.
