It comes before the thermal warnings (11.506 / 11.543 / 12.060 s) and the FW DVFS step-down (11.813 s), so neither of those can have caused it. The failure reason logged is rx_detect_fail. No EQ phase, eq_fail or ASPM L1 marker appears, which argues against EQ tuning and ASPM causes. The failure affects the whole link, not one lane.
